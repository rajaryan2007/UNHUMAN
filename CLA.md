# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

*Template. This is a starting point, not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it
before you rely on it, and fill in the bracketed fields. The purpose is stated
below in plain terms; the binding text starts at "Agreement".*

**Why this exists:** the project is distributed under the MIT license, but the
maintainer may later want to offer it under other terms (for example a commercial
license, a source-available license such as BSL, or an open-core split). That is
only possible if the maintainer holds the necessary rights to every contribution.
This agreement grants those rights while leaving you free to use your own
contribution however you like. Signing it does not transfer ownership of your
work; it grants a broad license.

## How to accept

**Individual.** Add the following to the pull request or issue that contains your
first Contribution, or to this document in your own fork, replacing the fields:

```
I have read and agree to the UNHUMAN Engine Contributor License Agreement.
Name: <full legal name>
GitHub username: <username>
Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

**Entity.** Contributions made on behalf of a company require the corporate
version of this agreement, signed by an authorized representative, which also
lists the employees and contractors covered. Open an issue titled
`Corporate CLA` to request it.

If you are contributing on your own behalf as an individual, the individual
acceptance above is sufficient.
